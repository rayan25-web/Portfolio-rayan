Project 1

PLC-Based Automated Storage and Retrieval System

A two-tower storage and retrieval prototype run by a Siemens S7-1200 PLC. Ladder logic in TIA Portal V20 drives X-axis and Y-axis motion, a box-pushing actuator and four storage positions, with proximity sensors and limit switches giving position feedback. A WinCC HMI provides operator control, and the logic was tested in PLCSIM Advanced.

05 / How the background works

The line behind this page

The animation fixed behind every section is a concept sketch of a servo-driven conveyor line, a pick-and-place arm and a truck being loaded. I built it to show how a sequenced line behaves. It is not a result from one of my projects, and the ASRS above is my real PLC project. The label at the bottom right of the screen names the state the line is in.

01

Servo drives

The four servo motors under the belt turn the rollers, and the belt carries motor units toward the pick position at a steady speed. When the line stops, the servos stop with it.

02

Detection

Units stop at the end of the belt. On a real line, a proximity sensor at that point would tell the controller that a unit is ready to be picked.

03

Pick and place

The arm moves over the unit, lowers, grips it, lifts, and swings to the next free slot on the truck. It lowers, releases and returns for the next one, filling four slots.

04

Truck full

When the fourth motor is placed, the truck is full. The conveyor and servo drives stop, and the truck drives away.

05

Restart

The line stays stopped while the truck is away. When the truck returns to the loading position, the count resets and the conveyor starts again.
